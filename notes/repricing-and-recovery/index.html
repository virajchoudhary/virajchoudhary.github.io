Repricing isn't parameter recovery

Calibration study-note excerpt · 2026

Getting a price close to a reference and recovering the right model parameters are not the same achievement. That distinction runs through this project.

The historical benchmark records 36/36 controlled cases passing against the independent reference. Those are numerical cases, not 36 test suites or a measurement of live market accuracy.

The frozen inverse comparison found that close repricing did not guarantee accurate parameter recovery. Traditional calibration outperformed the neural models in that synthetic comparison.
