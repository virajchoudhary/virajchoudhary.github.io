AtlasOps

2026 · Research continuation

An agent proposing a repair, a person approving it, and software verifying recovery are different responsibilities. My work made those decisions explicit and reviewable.

The question

An incident workflow needs to record what was proposed, what was approved, and which evidence supports the decision. A general approval flag is not enough to describe a particular action.

The approach

I extended the inherited four-agent coordinator with exact-action operator approvals, Kubernetes resource preconditions, workload-bound recovery verification, and a durable incident journal.

Authenticated Operations APIs and redacted audit evidence expose that workflow. Wrong model identities, malformed replies, unsafe approval outcomes, and uncertain mutations are rejected rather than treated as successful recovery.

Implementation

  1. Incident workflow

    The inherited multi-agent platform proposes actions and collects incident context.

  2. Authenticated API

    Operations endpoints expose incident history and exact-action approvals through authenticated contracts.

  3. Guarded action

    Operator approval and resource preconditions bind a proposed repair to its intended Kubernetes workload.

  4. Recovery evidence

    Independent workload-bound verification, a durable incident journal, and source-labelled metrics/traces make the outcome inspectable.

  5. Regression checks

    Model-free software tests cover rejection paths, evidence integrity, and operational contracts.

My part in the work

The platform originated upstream and continued as university-team research. My merged contribution is the governed operational workflow, authenticated API, incident evidence, and related tests in PR #243, not authorship of the whole platform.

Python, FastAPI, Kubernetes contracts, OTLP telemetry, regression tests

Results and limits

PR #243 is merged and authored by virajchoudhary. Its retained full local suite records 3,902 passes and 51 skips; later focused operational checks record 220 passes. These are separate historical, model-free software runs, not fresh tests or a certified live recovery.

The implemented approval, precondition, and recovery-verification contracts are available in the merged source. Software completion does not resolve the scientific gates below.
